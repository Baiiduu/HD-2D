usage: meowart_api.py remove-background-run [-h] [--output-dir OUTPUT_DIR]
                                            --image-file IMAGE_FILE
                                            [--mode {pixel,hd}]
                                            [--quality {standard,advanced}]
                                            [--source-background-color SOURCE_BACKGROUND_COLOR]
                                            [--remove-bg-batch-size {2,4,8,16}]
                                            [--preserve-translucency | --no-preserve-translucency]

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --output-dir, --output_dir OUTPUT_DIR
                        Output root; the runner creates one task subdirectory
  --image-file IMAGE_FILE
                        Image or MP4 input; GIF, WebP and MP4 return lossless
                        WebP (no audio)
  --mode {pixel,hd}     Source artwork type
  --quality {standard,advanced}
                        Pixel: standard general (default), advanced complex
                        (max 32 frames). HD: standard budget (2
                        credits/batch), advanced general (default, 5
                        credits/batch). Pixel animation frames must be at most
                        256x256; static images have no 256x256 restriction.
  --source-background-color SOURCE_BACKGROUND_COLOR
                        Solid source background color used by Pixel advanced;
                        defaults to white
  --remove-bg-batch-size {2,4,8,16}
                        Frames per removal call; HD recommends 4; ignored for
                        Pixel advanced
  --preserve-translucency, --no-preserve-translucency
                        Keep soft alpha in Pixel removal; HD always keeps soft
                        alpha; no extra credits
